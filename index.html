The Festival

Not a rum tour.
A rum movement.

Manchester has its hall. Barbados has its rum shop culture. St. Croix has something neither of them has — 265 years of active distilling on the same soil, a living Afro-Caribbean heritage woven into every pour, and streets that were built for celebration.

Cruzan Rum Fest fills the gap the Caribbean has been missing. Two days. Multiple venues across Christiansted. 30+ Caribbean rum brands. Master blender sessions. Street food. Quelbe. The kind of hospitality that makes people miss flights.
